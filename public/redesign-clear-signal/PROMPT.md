# TezHR marketing redesign — Clear Signal

## Improved master prompt

Redesign TezHR’s marketing website as one coherent, Awwwards-calibre corporate product system—not a visual experiment and not a generic AI SaaS template.

TezHR is an applicant-tracking system built for hiring in Uzbekistan. It turns large, multilingual applicant pools into a clear, evidence-backed shortlist in seconds. It understands Uzbek, Russian, and English, sources candidates from branded application pages, an owned talent pool, hh.uz, and public Telegram channels, and always leaves the final decision to a human recruiter.

Create a consistent set of high-fidelity 1536 × 1024 desktop marketing-page concepts. Balance approximately 70% commercial clarity with 30% creative distinction. Every page must feel unmistakably TezHR and part of the same website.

### Creative direction

- Central brand idea: **from applicant noise to a clear hiring signal**.
- Use one dominant visual idea and one believable product proof per page.
- Use generous whitespace, calm pacing, a disciplined grid, and no more than two major compositional zones above the fold.
- Keep one shared header: TezHR; Product, Sourcing, Security, About; RU / UZ / EN; and “Start free”.
- Palette: warm near-white, deep ink, signal blue, and human amber only for recruiter decisions.
- Typography: one modern neo-grotesk superfamily; readable regular copy and mildly condensed display weight.
- Recurring signature: one thin blue “signal path” connecting evidence and decisions.
- Use realistic product proof: multilingual CV evidence, ranking rationale, local sourcing channels, permissions, and recruiter control.
- Keep every claim truthful. AI may recommend and organize; it must never reject a candidate automatically.

### Avoid

Generic AI gradients, glowing orbs, robots, brains, sparkles, cosmic motifs, purple haze, glassmorphism, card overload, bento grids, poster density, paper collage, decorative charts, fake logos, fake testimonials, fake metrics, stock startup scenes, and excessive borders.

### Pages

1. Home — applicant volume resolves into a five-person shortlist.
2. Product — CV evidence explains strengths, gaps, and role fit.
3. Sourcing — branded apply page, talent pool, hh.uz, and Telegram become one pipeline.
4. Local intelligence — Uzbek, Russian, and English evidence resolves into one hiring context.
5. Security — access, permissions, activity, and human approval stay visible.
6. About — an editorial Tashkent workplace image grounds the product locally.
7. Contact — a focused walkthrough request starts with one open role.

### Output

One polished full-bleed 1536 × 1024 desktop page image per concept, readable English UI text, no browser chrome or device frame, and enough of the next section to establish page pacing.
